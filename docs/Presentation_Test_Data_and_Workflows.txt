KEY RULES TO MENTION
- Only teachers ticked for a subject can be chosen for it; one teacher per subject per class.
- Submitted marks are read-only; published marks change only through an approved correction.
- Payments are never edited or deleted; paying more than owed is kept as credit; dates use the Malawi date.
- Guardians see results only when the balance is zero or in credit.
- Progression happens only after Term 3 results are posted; earlier missing terms count as absent.
- Form 4 students cannot be promoted (there is no Form 5).
- Relationships: Mother, Father, Sister, Brother, Grandmother, Grandfather, Auntie, Uncle, Guardian, Other.
